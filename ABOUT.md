## Goals
I want to become a physician who brings the discipline of safety engineering to patient care. My first career, as a cybersecurity engineer at a national laboratory and as a security engineer at the U.S. Department of State, trained me to look for how systems fail, to design for human error and to keep an honest record of what went wrong and why.

Full-time patient care at the University of Washington, and shadowing across anesthesiology, surgery, critical care, and palliative and end-of-life care, has shown me where those habits matter most: in the moments when a patient's safety depends on a team getting the details right. I am drawn especially to anesthesiology, critical care and palliative medicine. I want to care for patients across Washington, including communities that face barriers to care.
