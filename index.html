Evidence & method

The reasoning should be visible.

Recommendations, established knowledge and fictional applications have different roles. The documentation makes that distinction explicit.

01 / Source

Identify the evidence.

Clinical sources are linked. Review records distinguish identifier checks, selected passage review and the limits of a broader appraisal.

02 / Interpret

Keep the scope intact.

Guideline recommendations retain their strength and patient population. General knowledge and context-dependent practice are labelled separately.

03 / Apply

Make the example honest.

Patients, numerical findings and decision trees are synthetic teaching material. They illustrate reasoning rather than a standardized treatment regimen.

About the portfolio

A record of study,
revision and curiosity.

Collin B. George’s independent preclinical study portfolio focuses on perioperative analgesia, critical care physiology and the interpretation of clinical evidence.

The work combines educational source analysis, synthetic case discussions and software exploration. It is presented as a learning record, with its contribution, use of AI assistance and review limitations stated openly.
